Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a big audience?

No. The system is designed around finding the right people, even if you are starting with zero followers.

Do I need to run ads?

No. You'll learn both free and paid ways to find customers. You can start with the methods that fit your business and budget.

What if I have zero customers right now?

That's exactly where this starts. The first goal is customer #1.

What if my product is B2B?

The system can be used for B2B as well as B2C. Your customer-finding method will change depending on who buys your product.

What if I'm not good at sales?

You don't need to become a natural salesperson. You'll get simple frameworks and scripts to help you start conversations and handle common objections.

Is 100 customers guaranteed?

No. Your results depend on your product, market, price, execution and customer demand. The goal is to give you a clear system and execution plan for getting there.
